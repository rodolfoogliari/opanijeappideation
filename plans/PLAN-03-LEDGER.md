## 2 · The three gates that can stop the project

Most of the plan is reversible. These three are not, and each has the authority to halt what follows.

### Gate 1 — Stage 2, row 55. *Can halt the launch.*

> **AMENDED 2026-08-05 (later) — the gate did not fire, but the founder acted as though it had.**
> Row 59 was one tester, not ten, on a play-layer-off build — formally a precursor, not Gate 1. The
> founder nonetheless suspended the tester programme and ordered the surface redone. **That is the
> gate working better than written:** the whole hazard PLAN-03 names below is that a bad first-session
> number gets rationalized away, and instead a single bad one was acted on immediately. The formal
> gate still stands for when Stage 2 resumes.

> **AMENDED 2026-08-05 — the precursor has already fired.** Row 59 is one unaided beginner on a real
> device scoring **0/10**. That is **not** Gate 1: row 55 is defined as ten testers on a
> play-layer-on build, and this was one tester on a play-layer-off build. Recording it as though the
> gate had fired would be the label upgrade METHOD forbids. **But dismissing it because n=1 is the
> same error facing the other way.** The plan's own words below — *"the gate most likely to be
> rationalized away"* — now apply before the gate rather than after it. Redesign the door first
> (INPUT-92) and spend the ten testers on the redesigned one.

**If fewer than 7 of 10 beginners get from voicing to playing on the screen drum in one sitting,
stop and redesign the door.** Do not proceed to distribution with a broken first session. This is the
gate most likely to fire and the one most likely to be rationalized away. Write the criterion down
*before* the testers arrive, so the number is judged rather than negotiated.

### Gate 2 — Stage 3, 100 completed first sessions. *Gates the money work.*
Stage 4 is 5–7 vibecoding weeks of seam-wiring plus a payment rail activation. **It is not worth
building for an audience that does not exist.** If Stage 3's distribution acts cannot produce 100
completed first sessions, the problem is distribution, and no amount of Stage 4 fixes it.

### Gate 3 — six months, 100 second sessions. *Governs whether this is a business.*
**Not installs. Not follows. Returns.** If six months of the free instrument, the web room, Junior's
network, the reels, the CRM outreach and YouTube search cannot produce 100 people who played twice,
then the app is not a standalone business — and it should settle honestly into being the immersion
funnel's trust surface, which is still worth having built at roughly the cost this plan spends.

**Write that down now, while it is cheap to say.** The purpose of naming a failure condition in
advance is that it is the only time you can name it honestly.

## 4 · The honesty rules

Carried from `METHOD.md` because they are the only thing that makes a ledger worth keeping.

1. **No row upgrades without dated evidence.** Ever.
2. **Never upgrade a label to make a plan read better.** This is the failure mode that produces a
   confident-looking estate with nothing underneath it.
3. **A worker's write-up is a claim, not evidence** — including Claude's, including a subagent's,
   including this document's. Verify before relying.
4. **An honest FALSIFIED is worth more than a hopeful ASSUMPTION.** Three rows are currently
   FALSIFIED (27, 29, 30) and the estate is stronger for saying so.
5. **Emulator evidence is not device evidence.** The two things only a human can verify — how the
   audio feels through a speaker and how touch latency feels under a finger — are founder-time,
   scheduled, never hoped for.
