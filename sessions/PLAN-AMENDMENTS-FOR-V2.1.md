**What this file is.** Everything from the 2026-07-31 conceptualization session that touches the
plan of record: divergences, promotions, and additions. Nothing here is operative. The companion

## 1 · Amendments, by section

### A1 · §4.1 — the door's arc no longer closes on the call

**Was.** Release 1 ships "the **Primeira Chamada** closing into the first ledger entry, written and
shown."

**Now.** The first entry is earned by the first real practice, not by answering the call (E5). The
arc is welcome → choose → call → play → entry.

**Why it matters.** The call and the entry are no longer the same moment, so the entry's threshold
becomes load-bearing on M1's headline metric. Set low enough to land inside the first sitting, the
door still closes in one visit; set high and the door does not close at all. The threshold is a
build-side decision, expressed in countable facts, informed by Junior.

### A2 · §4.1 and §6 — Salvador moves behind the master's invitation

**Was.** Release 1 ships "the immersion's trust surface: the actual named battery you would sit
inside in Salvador, consultation booking, no checkout." §6 and D3 open M4's runway at M1 on that
basis.

**Now.** Salvador appears at the ending rather than as a permanent surface (E10), reached through
the master's next-step invitation branched by language.

**Why it matters, and why the runway survives.** The founder's instinct is sound — a permanent
"book Salvador" tab cheapens a line that closes on trust, and only *trust-sold, consultation-closed*
is RULED, not the surface's placement. But the free course is Portuguese and domestic (A7) while the
season is USD and international, so an ending reachable only through that course would hide the
immersion from the people who buy it. Routing E7's invitation by language keeps D3's runway open at
near-zero cost.

**Watch item.** Locale branching is a two-way branch, not the offer engine (which §4.1 defers to
Release 2). It should not be allowed to grow into one.

### A3 · §7.2 rank 6 and INPUT-14 — subtitling promoted from budget call to Release 1 blocker

**Was.** English subtitling is "a *schedule* constraint on Release 1's international trust surface,
not an irrecoverability one," and INPUT-14 is a founder budget call.

**Now.** E11 makes language follow the phone. An English-set phone therefore receives an English
interface wrapped around Junior's Portuguese welcome, Portuguese call, Portuguese invitation and —
under E14 — Portuguese narration. Without subtitles the door is broken for exactly the audience that
buys the season.

**Status.** Still not irrecoverable (post-production remains available at similar cost). But it is
now structural to Release 1 rather than optional to it, and belongs in M1's cost lines (§7.4)
alongside storage and counsel.

### A4 · §7.2 and the pre-shoot list (D14) — four recording categories added to M0

Near-zero cost while the cameras are rolling, unrecoverable afterwards. Ranked with the existing
scope, all four sit at rank 3 by irrecoverability.

| Recording | Source decision |
|---|---|
| Entrance calls and count-ins, per rhythm, per incoming part | E1 |
| The welcome — one, durable, price-free | E4 |
| The next-step invitation — two versions, domestic and Salvador, both price-free | E7, A2 |
| Narrated video, per catalog item | E14 |

**A production rule attaches to all of them.** No recording of a master may name a price or a
product. Pricing is the founder's and moves (§12); a recording that names a figure expires the day
it does.

### A5 · §3.5 and INPUT-21 — partition capture extends to narration items

**Was.** The five-value partition is captured item by item at the shoot, with playable material in
view.

**Now.** E14 makes narrated video a first-class content type, and narration is where liturgical
meaning sits most directly — orixá association, ceremonial function, what a toque is for. The
partition must be captured on narration items too, at the same shoot, with the same five values.

**Why it matters.** A rhythm may be safely playable-inside while an account of what it is for is
archive-only. Nothing in the current capture design distinguishes them.

### A6 · §4.1 — a bought course is lessons, rooms and narration

**Was.** "The existing course catalog folded in as owned rooms where migration is cheap — the
Netflix clone stops being the product and becomes a shelf inside it."

**Now.** E14 makes the bought line three things at once: lessons to watch, rooms to play, and the
master's narration. The existing course infrastructure becomes the lesson half rather than dead
weight — a better outcome for the estate than v2.0 assumed.

**Cost consequence.** Lessons and narration sit on top of the ladder's media, which enlarges
INPUT-48 a second time. INPUT-48 already gates M1's costing; it now gates it against a larger
number.

### A7 · §9 and §18.1 — permanent free users become an operations line

**Was.** The −R$60k operations line does not carry the recurring infrastructure the ladder requires;
v2.0 already flags storage and CDN as missing from it.

**Now.** E13 creates permanently entitled, zero-revenue users with a non-zero bandwidth cost, for
life, by charter (red line #5).

**Scale, honestly.** Small. It is the same ~24 files forever, so caching flattens the marginal cost
toward nothing after a user's first weeks. It should appear in the monthly cash model rather than be
discovered in it.

**The consequence that actually matters is strategic:** conversion now runs on desire and never on
deprivation. That is the only version consistent with the rest of the architecture, and it should be
stated as a design constraint, not left as an implication.

### A8 · §4.1 and D27 — the cache/library boundary needs stating

**Was.** D27's mitigation notes that "aggressive HTTP caching covers most of the gap" left by
deferring offline download. The boundary between the two is not drawn anywhere.

**Now.** E16 relies on that caching for practice to survive a dropped connection, which makes the
boundary load-bearing. Proposed language: **a cache is ephemeral, invisible, OS-evicted and carries
no user-facing list; a download is durable, visible and enumerable.** The session survives a tunnel;
nobody acquires a permanent copy.

**Why it matters.** The moment a cached copy becomes durable and visible it is a download, and
INPUT-47 — whether red line #6 must reach already-downloaded copies — returns to the Release 1
critical path that D27 removed it from. Counsel should be asked whether a session-scoped cache is a
copy for red line #6's purposes; it is a small addition to a brief already being written.

## 2 · One finding that needs the founder's attention

**Under E17, the company's most public surface is built on Junior's material.**

The free door is what everyone sees, what gets screenshotted, and what has maximum reach precisely
because it costs nothing. Risk #2 — gamification read as commodifying the sacred — is named in v2.0
as "the estate's most exposed risk under A13." E17 places that risk at the point of maximum
visibility.

**This is not a problem, and it may be the opposite.** A13 rules that candomblé rhythms circulate
secularly in Brazil and are in scope; INPUT-20's Reading A, if assented, puts the rhythm as a
musical object outside red line #1. And risk #2's stated mitigation is structural: *Junior's
first-person authorship visible on the material, his name on the partition, in his voice.* This
session's decisions deliver exactly that — E4's welcome, E1's entrance calls, E7's invitation and
E14's narration put Junior's voice through the entire free door. The mitigation is now stronger
than it was when the risk was written.

**What follows is narrow and specific.** The free rhythm's partition value stops being a routine
catalog ruling and becomes Release-1-critical. It should be taken to Junior explicitly, as its own
question, alongside INPUT-41 at the shoot — not gathered anonymously with the rest of INPUT-21.
