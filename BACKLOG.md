# Opanijé — Backlog

**Status.** The working surface. Constituted 2026-08-03 under **D83** (Game Addendum 04 §7), which
split the charter from the backlog and put a door label on every open item.

**What this file is.** Everything open, who owns it, what it blocks, and whether it can be undone.
Nothing constitutional lives here — that is `CHARTER.md`. Nothing decided lives here — that is
`registers/`.

**The door rule.** **ONE-WAY** items get the formal treatment: a written question, three options with
pros and cons, the owner's explicit answer. **TWO-WAY** items are decided by default and revised on
evidence. The test for a hard case: *what does it cost to undo in six months?* If the answer involves
another shoot day, another person's signature, or a promise already made to a student — it is one-way.

**The ageing rule.** No input ages past thirty days. The monthly decision hour (`METHOD.md` §3) exists
to drain this register on a rhythm rather than on a deadline.
