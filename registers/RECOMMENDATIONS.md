# Opanijé — Recommendation Register (R1–R88)

**Status.** Register of record for this series as of 2026-08-03, under D83 (Addendum 04 §7).
**Supersedes.** The per-session tables in `sessions/` for this series, which remain readable as
derivation history but are no longer the register.
**Range.** R1 through R88.
**Rule.** Rows are appended, never rewritten. A row's status may change; its claim may not.

---

A recommendation is the contracted PM's advice. It is the weakest instrument in the estate and it
is meant to be: it carries no force of its own, binds nobody, and becomes operative only when the
founder adopts it, when a delta promotes it, or when a founder ruling closes the input it points
at. A recommendation that is never taken is not a failure of the register — it is the register
working, because the advice and the decision stay visibly separate. Where a recommendation was
adopted, the adopting instrument is named in the Status cell; where it was overtaken, the
superseding delta is named. Nothing here is measured evidence and no recommendation outranks a red
line.

**Status vocabulary.** ADOPTED — taken, and operative through the named instrument. STRONG /
Pre-shoot, strong / Build-side, strong — the issuing document's own emphasis, preserved verbatim.
ACCEPTED. OPEN — issued, awaiting the founder, usually through a named INPUT. SUPERSEDED BY Dxx.
WITHDRAWN. REVISED / AMENDED where a later document changed the advice without withdrawing it.
Conditional statuses ("Strong; blocked only by INPUT-72") are the working state and are quoted as
written.

**Door labels** (D83 item 2). ONE-WAY — expensive or impossible to reverse: consent scope,
anything given to the free tier (red line #5 makes it permanent), the fixed reference dialect,
publication of the standard, and anything shot at M0. TWO-WAY — revisable on evidence. Labels
taken from the estate's own reasoning where it states one; otherwise marked "(derived)". Closed and
adopted rows carry "—".

**R1–R44 are carry-ins and their originating text is not in the estate.** They were issued by
`PRODUCT-GOALS-VNEXT-CONSOLIDATED-V1.md`, which v2.0 consolidated and superseded on 2026-07-30; no
copy survives in the tree or in git history. v2.0 carries them by citation rather than by table —
§18.2 is titled "Recommendations issued **by this file**" and starts at R45. Each R1–R44 row below
therefore states the claim **as v2.0 and its successors attest it**, cited to the section that
attests it. Eight numbers in that block are attested nowhere and are flagged as gaps rather than
invented. This is recorded, not repaired: a reconstructed claim is honest only if it says it is one.
