# Opanijé — Risk Register (#1–#37)

**Status.** Register of record for this series as of 2026-08-03, under D83 (Addendum 04 §7).
**Supersedes.** The per-session tables in `sessions/` for this series, which remain readable as
derivation history but are no longer the register.
**Range.** #1 through #37. #1–#29 issued to 2026-08-03; #30–#37 added 2026-08-05.
**Rule.** Rows are appended, never rewritten. A row's status may change; its claim may not.

---

The risk register is where the estate writes down what it is exposed to before the exposure argues
for itself. **No risk in this range has been retired.** Four have been materially re-rated without
being re-numbered — #11, #18, #23 and #25 — and a re-rating that keeps its number is the easiest
thing in the estate to miss, so each says so in its own row. Every risk's mitigation is carried as
the issuing document stated it; where a later document weakened, strengthened or restated that
mitigation, the change is in *Status* and the original stands in *Mitigation*.

**Wording.** Risk text is not softened. Where the estate wrote a failure mode in the harshest
available terms — *the failure mode is not a bad Release 1; it is no Release 1* — the harsh version
is the one recorded.

**Epistemic labels.** Risks themselves carry no epistemic label. Where a risk's mitigation rests on
a labelled claim, that label is carried verbatim from the issuing document: HYPOTHESIS (#8, #25),
VERIFIED-INTERNAL (#10), DERIVED (#18), RULED (#27). No label here has been invented or upgraded.

**Ratification state.** Risks enter by delta. **D19** (risks #16–#20 added; risk #3 amended) is one
of D1–D19, which v2.0 §16 and §0.3 carry as **assumed unratified** — operative as plan-of-record and
reversible at review. **D47** (risk #22) is **operative on issue** under Mandate 01 §6. Rows #23–#29
enter through the game addenda at the status their sections state.

**Door labels** (D83 item 2). ONE-WAY — expensive or impossible to reverse: consent scope, anything
given to the free tier (red line #5 makes it permanent), the fixed reference dialect, publication of
the standard, and anything shot at M0. TWO-WAY — revisable on evidence. Labels are taken from the
estate's own reasoning where it states one; otherwise derived from the five one-way classes above
and marked "(derived)". On a risk row the door attaches to the **commitment that creates the
exposure**, not to the mitigation, which is normally revisable. The column is carried here to match
`LEDGER.md`, `DELTAS.md` and `INPUTS.md`, which all place it immediately before the source.
