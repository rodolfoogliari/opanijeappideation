**What a decision is, and how it differs from everything else in this directory.** A decision is
something the estate has actually settled — by the founder, by Junior as the tradition's named
authority, or by a derivation nobody has contested. It is not advice (`RECOMMENDATIONS.md`), not a
change to the plan of record (`DELTAS.md`), not an open question (`INPUTS.md`), and not an untested
claim (`LEDGER.md`). Four series live here because the estate opened four, each in a different room
of the conversation, and never merged them.

**The four series, and what separates them.**

- **A1–A17 · founder rulings.** The oldest layer, ratified 2026-07-29 and carried into v2.0
  "unedited" (§0). They are governance and business-shape rulings, not product-form ones. Every one
  carries **RULED**.
- **S1–S9 · session decisions.** One sitting, 2026-07-31, second session. Sequencing, commerce, and
  the two decisions about Junior himself. Every one carries **FOUNDER-DECIDED**.
- **G1–G26 · game decisions.** The spine. Four addenda across four days, from vocalization-as-notation
  to the screen drum to the grading constitution. Labels are mixed and the mix is the point:
  FOUNDER-DECIDED, FOUNDER-FACT, DERIVED, PM-RECOMMENDED, and — from 2026-08-03 — MASTER-CONFIRMED.
- **E1–E21 · experience decisions.** What the student meets. E1–E19 from the Experience Spec, E20
  from Addendum 03, E21 from Addendum 04.

**Letter collisions, stated once so no reader has to guess.** Founder rulings are **A + number** and
are unrelated to **Fork A** (`FORKS.md`) and to the eight amendment sections **A1–A8** inside
`sessions/PLAN-AMENDMENTS-FOR-V2.1.md`, which are section IDs and not rulings — that file's own A5,
A6 and A7 headers describe partition capture, bought courses and free-tier operations, while its
prose separately cites the *rulings* A7, A12 and A13. Game decisions are **G + number** and are
unrelated to the **Gate criteria G0–G7** (v2.0 §8), which are always cited "Gate G0". The
§7.9 grant-track bullets lettered "A1:" and "A2:" are grant rules, not rulings. See `NUMBERING.md`.

**Epistemic labels are carried verbatim from the issuing document.** Nothing here is upgraded,
softened, or paraphrased into a different label. Where a decision's status was later amended,
narrowed, voided or restored, the *Status* cell records the **later** state and names what it was —
the estate's authority order, later over earlier, with Game Addendum 04 (2026-08-03) governing
anything it touches.

**Door labels** (D83 item 2). ONE-WAY — expensive or impossible to reverse: consent scope, anything
given to the free tier (red line #5 makes it permanent), the fixed reference dialect, publication of
the standard, and anything shot at M0. TWO-WAY — revisable on evidence. Labels are taken from the
estate's own reasoning where it states one; otherwise marked "(derived)". Closed and ratified rows
carry "—".

**Three numbers in the A-series are not in the estate.** A9, A11 and A16 appear nowhere in any
document — not in v2.0, not in any session file, not in any register. They are emitted below as
rows and flagged, never skipped and never invented.
