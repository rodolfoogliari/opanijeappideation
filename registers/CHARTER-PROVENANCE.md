# Opanijé — Charter Provenance

**Status.** Register of record for this series as of 2026-08-03, under D83 (Addendum 04 §7).
**Supersedes.** The per-session tables in `sessions/` for this series, which remain readable as
derivation history but are no longer the register.
**Range.** charter material.
**Rule.** Rows are appended, never rewritten. A row's status may change; its claim may not.

---

**What this file is.** The extraction pass that feeds the two-page Charter required by D83
(Addendum 04 §7 item 1). It gathers the constitutional material — the one law, the six red lines,
the partition, consent-and-credit, the two-ledger architecture, the authority order, the
prohibition list, the governance split, and the two standing sequencing rules — with **wording
quoted exactly** and every row carrying its source.

**Why wording is the whole point here.** Everything else in the estate may be summarised. Charter
material may not. Red line #5 makes anything given permanent, red line #1 reserves form to a named
authority, and the Mandate's withdrawal of a large body of PM-derived invariants only worked
because the withdrawn text was demonstrably *not* charter. A Charter that paraphrases will
re-adopt what was withdrawn or narrow what was ruled. Where this file quotes, the quote is exact;
where it derives, the derivation is marked.

**Door labels.** Assigned per D83 item 2. The estate's own one-way list is: consent scope, anything
given to the free tier, the fixed reference dialect, publication of the standard, and anything shot
at M0. Labels taken from the estate's own reasoning are unmarked; labels reasoned from that list
carry "(derived)". Closed or ratified rows carry "—".

**Row count.** 89 rows, CH-01 through CH-89, contiguous.

## 2 · The six red lines

**Source.** v2.0 §2. The preamble sentence, which the Charter should keep because it states where
the red lines are meant to land: "The six red lines, to be written into the corporate documents
before any first check clears:"

> 1. **Each tradition's sacred boundary is governed by that tradition's own named authority.** For
>    Ketu Candomblé material: Junior, always. No game mechanics on sacred material unless its
>    authority designs the form himself; no sacred iconography in product art without approval.
> 2. **Standing is only earned.** No entry, passage, honour, or acknowledgment is ever purchasable.
> 3. **Supply is curated.** Masters enter by vouched invitation through a bench; no self-serve door.
> 4. **No synthetic voice, ever.** Every voice is a real recorded human; no cloned masters, no
>    machine-composed calls "in his style."
> 5. **The free tier's permanent-access promise holds.** The house never takes back what it gave —
>    and this attaches to anything the app gives away, including the Room's free set (R24/R30).
> 6. **Consent and credit are structural.** A teller's withdrawal takes his material down; credit is
>    permanent; price worlds never cross-wire.

Closing sentences of §2, also charter-level and also quoted exactly:

> An investor unwilling to sign these is buying a different company, and the answer to that investor
> is no. A milestone missed is a milestone missed; a red line crossed is the company ended.

**The one red line with no operating form.** v2.0 §2 states it plainly and the Charter must not

> **One red line has no operating form yet.** Red line #6 requires that a withdrawal takes material
> down, and as of 2026-07-30 there is no cross-system takedown anywhere in the estate
> (VERIFIED-INTERNAL; ledger row 30, FALSIFIED). **This is not a violation** — no consented material

> line's operating form rather than as a feature (§4.1). Whether the promise must reach copies

### 2.1 · Cross-check against OPERATOR-MANDATE-01 §4 — "the four boundaries that are charter"

**This is the most easily mis-read relationship in the estate, and the Charter depends on getting
it right.** Mandate 01 (2026-07-31, FOUNDER-DECIDED, operative on issue) withdrew a large body of
PM-derived invariants. Its §4 is titled "The four boundaries that are charter, and what they
actually permit". The Charter must not read "four" as a reduction of "six".

**How §4's four relate to the six red lines.** §4 has four *subsections*, which between them cover
**five** red lines — because §4.4 covers two at once:

| Mandate §4 subsection | Red line(s) covered |
|---|---|
| §4.1 · Standing is not currency | red line #2 |
| §4.2 · Nothing is taken back | red line #5 |
| §4.3 · Sacred material's form is Junior's | red line #1 |
| §4.4 · Real voices, and takedown reaches everything | red lines #4 **and** #6 |

**Red line #3 (supply is curated) is absent from Mandate §4** — not withdrawn, not narrowed, simply
not engaged, because §4's scope is engagement design and red line #3 governs how masters enter the
bench. Nothing in the Mandate touches it. The Charter carries all six.

**What the Mandate says about its own authority**, quoted because it is the sentence that keeps the
withdrawal below the charter:

> **Authority.** Product form on secular material, brand, voice and product shape are the founder's
> under v2.0 §12 (RULED). The prohibitions withdrawn below were **PM-derived invariants and adopted
> recommendations, never charter**. Withdrawing them requires no ratification from anyone.

> **Nothing here touches a red line.** §4 states exactly which four constraints are charter, why they
> survive this mandate, and what they permit — which is far more than the withdrawn text assumed.

**The Charter must not re-adopt what §2 of the Mandate withdrew.** The withdrawn text is listed in
Mandate 01 §2 by citation and includes Experience Spec §3 invariants 1, 2, 3 and 6, Experience Spec
§5's "No streaks, targets, scores, or verdicts — structurally, not by restraint", E9 and E12 as
originally written, the addendum's §7.1 prohibition half, §7.7 and §7.8, and D28/R51. **None of it
was charter.** A Charter drafted from the Experience Spec's invariant list rather than from v2.0 §2
would silently reinstate a withdrawn PM over-reading.

**What the Mandate expressly leaves standing**, quoted:

> **Unchanged and still operative:** every red line; v2.0 §3.3's two-layer architecture; E8 (parts
> switchable freely, both directions, any time); E13 (the free room, forever, unchanged); R53 (no
> recording of a master names a price or a product).

## 3 · The partition

**Source.** v2.0 §3.5, "The sacred partition (R20 — ADOPTED, five values; enforcement language
corrected — D17)".

> A per-catalog-item field, **set by Junior**:
>
> *playable-inside · gamifiable (recognition/vocalization only) · teachable-but-not-gamifiable ·
> archive-only · unrecordable*

**Where it is enforced.** v2.0 corrects v1.0's wording and the correction must survive into the
Charter verbatim, because the difference is a design constraint, not a nuance:

> **Where it is enforced, stated accurately.** v1.0 said "enforced at the data layer." The audit
> found that ACF already supports a five-value single-select per item and the dormant catalog code
> defines fields of exactly that shape — but WordPress post meta is schemaless and enforces nothing
> at the database level. The accurate statement, and the one this plan now uses: **enforced at the
> API contract layer, validated on read, with no client-side override path — which holds so long as
> every read path goes through the API.** That is a real protection and a design constraint on every
> future surface; it is not a database guarantee, and nobody should later rely on wording that
> implies one.

> "Playable-inside" is added (per Session 03 §3) because a student occupying an ensemble role inside
> a recorded liturgical battery is a stronger form question than a recognition quiz. Values are
> captured item by item **at the M0 shoot** (INPUT-21) — the rulings can come later, the shoot's
> item-by-item context cannot.

**A second use for the same field (R46).** Offline availability, when it ships, is made a function
of the partition value — "sacred-side items stream only, secular items may be downloaded" — which
"ties protection to the actual harm — Junior's material in a nightclub edit, a red-line-#1 problem
rather than a revenue one — instead of to revenue, and reuses a field that already exists."

**The governance fork, open.** Quoted in full because the Charter must record that the partition's
reach is not settled:

> **The governance fork (open — INPUT-20).** Red line #1 requires that no game mechanics be placed
> on sacred material unless its authority designs the form himself. Reading A: the rhythm as a
> musical object sits outside red line #1 (the founder designs the form) while the liturgical layer
> around it stays inside (orixá association, ceremonial function, ceremony-only variants, songs and
> language, consecration status of the drums — Junior designs a form or excludes, item by item).
> Reading B: the item stays inside entirely and Junior designs the form. **The plan assumes Reading
> A pending Junior's assent**; the practical difference is whether the game's form is blocked on his
> creative bandwidth or only on his partition rulings — a game fed by the whole catalog versus part
> of it. The standing partition either way: **gamify the secular, archive the sacred**, boundary set
> item by item — and its mitigation of risk #2 must be structural: Junior's first-person authorship
> visible on the material, his name on the partition, in his voice.

## 5 · The two-ledger architecture

**Source.** Mandate 01 §4.1, "Standing is not currency (red line #2)". The table is the estate's
canonical statement and should be carried into the Charter as a table:

> **Two ledgers, kept structurally apart:**
>
> | | The play layer | The Caderno |
> |---|---|---|
> | Content | XP, streaks, levels, goals, cosmetics, celebration | *Received*, *Heard*, *Confirmed* |
> | Author | The app | Human events and the master |
> | Character | Soft, resettable, loud, playful | Permanent, quiet, withdrawal-proof |
> | May be gamified | Entirely | **Never** |
> | May be bought | No (nothing in the app is bought except access) | No — charter |

**Why the separation is structural, not stylistic.** Three statements, each from a different
document, and together they are the argument:

- **The build rule (R59), Mandate 01 §4.1:** "the play layer is a separate, resettable subsystem from
  day one and never writes into the Caderno's tables. This is free to do now, while the rail has zero
  rows, and expensive to unpick later."
- **The reason the prohibition could be withdrawn safely, Mandate 01 §3:** "**The Caderno is written by
  human-shaped events**, not by the play layer (§4.1). Standing has its own ledger and the engagement
  layer cannot reach it."
- **The separation becoming load-bearing, Addendum 04 §3.3 Level 3:** "The play ledger stays resettable
  by design — the two-ledger architecture was built for exactly this and is finally load-bearing."

**The underlying two-layer architecture it rests on** is v2.0 §3.3 (R28 — ADOPTED), which Mandate 01
§2 narrowed "as to *surfacing and celebration*" only, recording that "Layer 1 / Layer 2 architecture
survives intact":

> - **Layer 1 — facts about the user's own play.** Minutes, cycles held, parts attempted, levels
>   reached, their take beside the master's *(this last arrives with Release 2 — D26 removes the
>   microphone from Release 1)*. Uncapped, automatable, safe: software may count facts. Layer 1 makes
>   a student visible; **it never entitles.**
> - **Layer 2 — human attestation.** A master's judgment. Capped by his hours, never computed.
>   Judgment is human, monthly, public, by name: the cycle's response (§4.3).

**The Caderno's own invariants**, v2.0 §3.4, "enforced in the API contract layer (§3.5)":

> - **A Confirmed entry carries no link to any payment record** (R4). G0's audit becomes a query.
> - **No user-claimed entries, ever.** Entries are system-generated from actual events;
>   confirmations flow only through the platform channel.
> - **Tradition-governed visibility.** Junior's partition governs what is visible, item by item;
>   the register cannot be complete and does not pretend to be.
> - **Withdrawal-proof.** An entry survives the disappearance of the material it points at
>   (red line #6: material comes down, credit is permanent).
> - **Record from day one, present later.** Lineage cannot be backfilled: Release 1 carries the
>   ledger and shows the first entry; the full presentation surface waits for Release 3.

**Confirmed allocation**, v2.0 §3.4: "Confirmed stays exactly where it was: allocated by Junior
alone (A5 — RULED), non-purchasable, the apex the Room can make more *likely* without ever
entitling."

**The rules that keep the boundary visible in the interface:**

- **R61 (Mandate 01 §8):** "Fractions and totals are permitted for rooms and play progress; forbidden
  for Caderno entries and anything reading as a master's acknowledgment."
- **R62 (Mandate 01 §8):** "No engagement mechanic renders on a Caderno surface. The Caderno stays
  quiet — its power is that it is the one place in the app that never shouts."
- **R86, the vocabulary rule (Addendum 04 §3.3):** "play-layer words are spatial and musical — rooms,
