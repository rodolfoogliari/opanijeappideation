# Opanijé — Fork Board (A–L)

**Status.** Register of record for this series as of 2026-08-03, under D83 (Addendum 04 §7).
**Supersedes.** The per-session tables in `sessions/` for this series, which remain readable as
derivation history but are no longer the register.
**Range.** Forks A through L.
**Rule.** Rows are appended, never rewritten. A row's status may change; its claim may not.

**What a fork is.** An open product question the game conversation works through, lettered on
opening and carried until it closes, defers, or is answered contingently. Forks are **bare letters**
(Fork A … Fork L). They are unrelated to contradictions (C + number) and to founder rulings
(A + number) — see `NUMBERING.md`.

**Current state is Addendum 04 §15.** Where §15 records a fork as "as Addendum 03 §18, unchanged",
the substance is restated below rather than left as a pointer.

surface Junior has not seen.
