The ledger is the estate's discipline against wishful thinking: every load-bearing claim carries a
status and the event that would convert it. **Nothing in this ledger is measured evidence unless the
row says MEASURED — and as of 2026-08-03, no row does.** Rows 48–51 exist specifically so that the
usability pilot and M1 are read as *tests* rather than as verdicts.

**Rule of the ledger, carried verbatim from v2.0 §14.** No row's status upgrades without a dated
measurement, and no successor document may cite a row above its current status. Falsified rows are
retained, not deleted, with the work that makes them true named beside them.

**Status vocabulary.** Statuses are the estate's own, taken verbatim from the issuing document and
defined in `../METHOD.md` §1: RULED · MASTER-CONFIRMED · FOUNDER-FACT · REPORTED · ASSUMPTION ·
HYPOTHESIS · MEASURED · VERIFIED-EXTERNAL · VERIFIED-INTERNAL · PM-ASSERTION · plus the ledger's own
UNRESOLVED, UNMEASURED and FALSIFIED (D20). VERIFIED-INTERNAL (D11) and FALSIFIED are preserved as
issued rather than mapped onto a shorter list; **all VERIFIED-INTERNAL findings are self-audit, not
independent audit** (v2.0 §0.1). No status on this page has been upgraded. Where a later document
resolves a row, the resolution is recorded in *Converts at* — the claim and the status stand as
issued.

**The counterfactual problem, stated once.** Rows 35, 38, 41–45 and 48–51 have no counterfactual
inside this plan: Release 1 ships once, and red line #5 forbids the degrading-free-tier control.
They are recorded as a designed first measurement, not as proof that anything worked.

**Door labels** (D83 item 2). ONE-WAY — expensive or impossible to reverse: consent scope, anything
given to the free tier (red line #5 makes it permanent), the fixed reference dialect, publication of
the standard, and anything shot at M0. TWO-WAY — revisable on evidence. Labels are taken from the
estate's own reasoning where it states one; otherwise derived from the five one-way classes above
and marked "(derived)". Rows that carry no open conversion event carry "—". On a ledger row the door
attaches to the commitment the row rests on, not to the measurement, which is always repeatable.

**Two reading collisions, per `NUMBERING.md`.** Rows 10, 12 and 15 convert at **Gate** criteria
(Gate G1, Gate G4, Gate G1–G5 — v2.0 §8); these are *not* the game decisions G1–G26. Row numbers
here are ledger rows and are unrelated to the R-series and the D-series.
