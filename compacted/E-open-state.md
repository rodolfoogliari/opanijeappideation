# Opanijé — Open State (compaction E)

Mobile app for learning Afro-Brazilian (Ketu Candomblé / Bahian) percussion. **Junior** is the named
tradition authority — sacred form is his, absolutely; the **founder** holds product, price, brand
and secular form; **counsel** holds legal; an AI pipeline plus one operator is the **build side**.
Registers dated 2026-08-03. **No ledger row is MEASURED.** Six red lines: (1) sacred form belongs to
its named authority, (2) standing is only earned, never bought, (3) supply is curated, (4) no
synthetic voice ever, (5) the free tier's grant is permanent, (6) consent and credit are structural.
Doors: **1W** = one-way (consent scope, anything given free, the fixed reference dialect,
publication of the standard, anything shot at M0); **2W** = revisable on evidence.
