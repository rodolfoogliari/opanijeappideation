# G — Research, Method, Engagement (compaction for design)

Product: **Opanijé**, mobile app for learning Afro-Brazilian (Ketu Candomblé) percussion. Adults only in Release 1. No microphone in Release 1 (D26) — the app is structurally **deaf** and cannot grade playing. Named tradition authority: **Junior** (Ketu material). Two structurally separate layers: the **play layer** (loud, resettable, gamifiable) and the **Caderno** (quiet, permanent, human-authored standing).

---

## 1 · Operator Mandate 01 — the engagement toolkit (FOUNDER-DECIDED 2026-07-31, operative on issue)

### Permitted and expected in Release 1
Streaks · daily goals · session targets · fractions and totals · XP · levels · celebration moments · variable and delayed reward · loss aversion · unlockables · cosmetic collection · notification craft · milestone drama · comparison-against-your-own-past · behaviour-based auto-advancement (the app may open the next room) · the rest of the modern retention repertoire.

The prior blanket prohibition ("no streaks, no targets, no scores, no badges, no progress bars, structurally, not by restraint") is **withdrawn**. It confused *who may confer standing* (charter) with *how the app may behave* (not charter).

### The line, in one sentence
> **The app never grades your drumming. It may celebrate everything else.**

Musical judgment stays in the student's ear, in the agogô, and in the master's attestation. **Behavioural facts** — you opened the app, played eleven cycles, returned on day seven, chose a harder room, fourteen days running — are the app's to count, dramatize, reward, and chase.

### 1.2 The exclusion list — what still must not be built
1. Anything purchasable conferring/accelerating/implying standing (RL#2).
2. Anything gating access to what was given — hearts, lives, energy, timers on the free room (RL#5).
3. Any machine verdict on musicianship (structurally impossible under D26).
4. Synthetic voice of any kind in any celebration asset (RL#4).
5. Badges, titles, ranks reading as master acknowledgment, or play-layer rank shown as lineage rank (RL#2).
6. Any master recording naming a price or product (also: no master's voice inside an upsell moment).
7. Any gamified surface that survives withdrawal of the material it points at (RL#6).

**Everything not on this list is permitted.** Arguable mechanics: build, mockup, take to Junior.

## 2 · Research findings — what has evidence, what does not

**Two numbers judge every game element: first-session completion and day-7 return.**

### 2.1 Intrinsic vs extrinsic in music practice — the central finding
- **The activity is its own reward (STRONG).** Groove/entrainment engages motor *and* reward circuitry (NAcc, caudate, putamen); musical pleasure causes striatal dopamine release (caudate in anticipation, NAcc at peak). Achievable **solo, with a recording** — no partner, no verdict.
- **So this product needs extrinsic scaffolding *less* than a language app.** Duolingo must manufacture reward because conjugating verbs isn't pleasurable; percussion plays on home field. Strongest strategic finding in the base.
- **Overjustification (STRONG, 128 experiments).** Tangible, expected, performance-contingent rewards undermine free-choice intrinsic motivation: engagement-contingent d=−0.40, completion-contingent d=−0.36, performance-contingent d=−0.28. **Positive informational feedback *enhances* it** (free-choice d=+0.33, interest d=+0.31) — validating the master's correction as the competence anchor.
- **Groove inverted-U (STRONG, replicated, not unanimous).** Medium syncopation → most desire to move and most pleasure; a tunable sweet spot exists. Dissenting: R²=.010 (2018), a 2024 null. **Never alter a toque's syncopation to optimize engagement.**
- **Synchrony dissociation.** Entraining to a *recording* delivers the hedonic/reward payoff fully but **not** social bonding — affiliation effects are unique to interpersonal synchrony. Relatedness must come from the real human master.
- **Dropout in instrumental learning.** ~50% quit by 17. Autonomous motivation protects (intrinsic r=−0.50, identified r=−0.33); **guilt-avoidance/introjected motivation predicts dropout (r=+0.17)** — direct evidence that reproach mechanics are associated with quitting.
- **Deliberate practice explains only 21% of performance variance in music** — do not over-promise skill from hours logged.

### 2.2 Gamification effect sizes

| Finding | Effect | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Gamification on cognitive learning | g=0.49 (0.30–0.69, k=19) | STRONG; only cognitive survives high-rigour subset |
| on motivation | g=0.36 (0.18–0.54) | STRONG |
| on behaviour | g=0.25 (0.04–0.46) | STRONG but least stable |
| on **intrinsic motivation** | g=0.26 (small) | MODERATE, high heterogeneity, self-report heavy |
| on autonomy / relatedness / competence | g=0.64 / g=1.78 (very wide CI, WEAK) / **g=0.28 (minimal)** | — |
| Points, levels, leaderboards | ↑ performance **quantity** only; **no** reliable effect on intrinsic motivation or competence | MODERATE |
| Badges, leaderboards, performance graphs | ↑ competence satisfaction + task meaningfulness | MODERATE |
| Avatars, stories, teammates | ↑ relatedness | MODERATE |
| Implementation intentions ("if-X-then-Y") | **d=0.65** | STRONG |
| Progress self-monitoring | **d=0.40**, larger when physically recorded | STRONG *but goal-linked* |
| Peak-end rule on retrospective evaluation | r=0.58; duration neglected; better-ending colonoscopy raised actual return, OR=1.41 | STRONG |
| Streaks | Intact logged streaks raise next behaviour vs broken; repair attenuates the break; streak incentives raise persistence via goal commitment. Duolingo's +14% D7 is **vendor-reported, not peer-reviewed** | MODERATE |
| Habit formation | Median 66 days to automaticity, range 18–254; ~half never reached it. **Missing a single day did not measurably impair habit formation** — undercuts the necessity of streaks | MODERATE, widely mis-cited |
| Loot boxes ↔ problem gambling | η²≈0.05, subgroup d≈0.28–0.57, **correlational** | STRONG on harm |

**Drivers are game *fiction* (narrative) and *social interaction*, not points/badges in isolation.** A single element is motivationally ambivalent — a leaderboard can satisfy competence while thwarting autonomy.

**Backfire is documented:** a gamified course with leaderboard/badges/coins produced *lower* intrinsic motivation, satisfaction and exam scores. Leaderboards embarrass low-rankers; high-rankers coast; neither motivation is intrinsic.

**Novelty decay (MODERATE):** impact declines from ~week 4, falls 2–6 weeks, partly recovers weeks 6–10. Purely extrinsic elements decay; intrinsically anchored designs overcome the drop-off — need-supportive gamification saw autonomous motivation *increase* over a semester.

**Net cost of forgoing extrinsic mechanics: real but smaller than practitioners assume.** Streaks are the biggest genuine loss; points/leaderboards/progress bars are weak and decay fastest. Need-supportive design **loses on D1/D7, wins on 3–12-month retention and wellbeing.** "Humane design is free" is *not* supported.

### 2.3 Folklore or contested — do not build on
- **"Dopamine hit" framing — WEAK/folklore.** Dopamine encodes reward-prediction error and *"wanting"*, not *"liking"*. Maximising wanting without liking = compulsive, low-satisfaction, regretted use.
- **Zeigarnik effect — CONTESTED/WEAK** (poor replication).
- **Octalysis, Hooked (Eyal), Fogg B=MAP — practitioner folklore**, no independent validation. PENS/SDT is the one framework with a deep empirical base.
- **Dynamic difficulty adjustment — CONTESTED**; no DDA method reliably beat static difficulty. Player-chosen difficulty is the defensible choice.
- **Rules/Goals + Challenge + Mystery as "the strongest spine" — overstated.** Top combination in a meta-analysis of 37 trials / 182 effect sizes, but the DV was *learning outcome*, not retention or enjoyment. TRANSFER prior only.
- **No universal ranking of "most addictive mechanics" exists.** Gaming-disorder research is interactionist and associative; describe **risk clusters**, not a league table.

### 2.4 Where the evidence is thin or absent (the estate says so explicitly)
1. **Target-free, comparison-free self-monitoring** — no meta-analysis isolates it; effect probably exists but weaker than d=0.40. The **biggest evidence gap**; measure in-house.
2. **Named recognition by an authority as a retention driver** — grounded theoretically, unquantified.
3. **No high-quality peer-reviewed effectiveness study of any commercial practice app** (Yousician, SmartMusic, Simply Piano).
4. **Entrainment-with-a-recording for *sustained retention*** — the reward finding is solid; the retention link is inferred.
5. **Prerecorded-master relatedness** — a product HYPOTHESIS; parasocial presence ≠ reciprocal relatedness.
6. Only **15 empirical studies** of digital game-based learning in music education exist (2011–2023). There is almost no high-quality evidence for an adult, single-player, microphone-free, culturally governed percussion product.
7. Heterogeneity across gamification meta-analyses is high (I² often >85%) — pooled estimates are directional, not precise.

**Standing caveat:** research narrows the design space; it does not choose the product. **Nothing should be built because it worked in a paper** — it still has to be measured.

### 2.5 The highest-risk design gaps
1. **The return problem (highest risk).** Need-supportive, low-pressure designs underperform on short-horizon re-engagement — exactly the gap streaks and notifications fill. Mitigate: implementation intentions at onboarding, non-reproachful cueing ("your battery is ready", never "you've fallen behind"), peak-end session design, shorter felt distance to the next master interaction.
2. **The competence-signal vacuum.** No points *and* no microphone means the app cannot tell the learner they are improving. Mitigate: let the activity signal competence (audible lock-in), scaffold within the groove sweet spot, use the master's informational positive feedback.
3. Novelty decay without content replenishment. 4. Over-reliance on a monthly master appointment — use his recorded voice, count-ins and calls between cycles.

### 2.6 Ranked remaining levers (strongest first)
1. Intrinsic reward of playing (groove/entrainment) — STRONG, large, durable. 2. SDT need support — STRONG. 3. Relatedness/recognition via the named master ("appointment" mechanic) — MODERATE-STRONG, protective against dropout. 4. Peak-end session design — STRONG. 5. Self-monitoring / pure counting — MODERATE. 6. Implementation intentions — STRONG, d=0.65. 7. Curiosity/discovery of new content as **epistemic** variable reward — the ethical substitute for variable-ratio: unlock by exploring, not by being assessed — MODERATE. 8. Autonomy over difficulty — MODERATE.

### 2.7 The proposed core game (HYPOTHESIS, not approved spec)
**Hold & Rejoin** (internal label, not product copy): *lock in → a master-authored perturbation (reference part drops, density rises, a call occurs, the form turns) → hold or respond → the reference returns at a musically valid boundary → the result becomes audible → choose the next condition.* No score, no verdict; the player interprets the result. Difficulty is a **state space, not a ladder** — axes: reference support, dropout length, auditory density, tempo, form volatility, role stability; none is universally "more advanced", and the player must move in all directions. Other forms in the space: Call Hunter, Battery Detective (the app may verify what the player *identified on screen*, never how they played), Seat Switch, Route Composer, Collective Battery. **Authorship rule:** "variable" means one of several complete master-approved scripts, never procedural splicing of governed material. The mystery is *which lawful event, and can I stay with it?* — never *what random prize will I get?*
**Test design:** do not ship the streak inside the first experiment meant to prove the game, or D7 cannot distinguish musical pull from counter-protection. Stage streak arms later: A neutral history / B repairable daily streak / C rolling player-chosen cadence. Measure return **after the first break**, D14, D30, full rounds (not notification opens), pressure/guilt, and voluntary replay once the counter is no longer salient.

## 3 · METHOD §1 — the epistemic label system (every claim carries one)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RULED** | A founder or governance decision. Needs no market validation. |
| **MASTER-CONFIRMED** | A claim about the tradition, its pedagogy or its material, confirmed by the tradition's named authority (Ketu: Junior). Sits **above** FOUNDER-FACT for tradition claims and closes the corresponding verification input (D71). |
| **FOUNDER-FACT** | A fact about tradition, market or community only the founder holds, asserted in session and treated as true (FF1–FF4). |
| **FOUNDER-DECIDED** | A product decision taken by the founder in session. |
| **ADOPTED** | A previously open recommendation now operative as plan-of-record, reversible at ratification review. |
| **PM-RECOMMENDED** | The contracted product manager's advice. Carries no force until adopted. |
| **PM-ASSERTION** | A PM claim from general knowledge, unverified. Requires founder, Junior or counsel confirmation before anything rests on it. |
| **DERIVED** | A consequence that follows necessarily from something already decided, stated so it is visible rather than discovered. |
| **REPORTED** | An asset asserted as existing; not independently audited. |
| **ASSUMPTION** | A modeled number awaiting measurement. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | A causal claim that must be tested. |
| **MEASURED** | Supported by completed transactions or observed cohorts. |
| **VERIFIED-EXTERNAL** | A third-party rule or fact checked against source on a stated date, carrying its own re-verification requirement. |
| **VERIFIED-INTERNAL** | A fact about the company's own estate, checked against the running system on a stated date by inspection rather than by reading documentation. All such findings to date are **self-audit, not independent audit**. |
| **FALSIFIED** | A ledger status: a claim checked against reality on a stated date and found untrue. **Retained** on the ledger, not deleted, with the work that would make it true named beside it. |

**As of 2026-08-03, nothing in the estate carries MEASURED.** No label upgrades without dated evidence; never upgrade a label to make a plan read better. Rows are appended, never rewritten — status may change, the claim may not.

**Second, research-only sub-scale** (say which scale you are on): **DIRECT-ADJACENT** (games or instrumental music practice, but not this product/tradition) · **TRANSFER** (educational, consumer-behaviour, habit or general psychology moved into this context) · **MECHANISTIC** (lab/neuroscience explaining a possible process, never predicting product behaviour) · **HYPOTHESIS** (product reasoning that must be tested in Opanijé).

## 4 · One-way vs two-way doors

**ONE-WAY — expensive or impossible to reverse.** Five named classes:
1. **Consent scope** — cannot be widened after the shoot without returning to the signatories.
2. **Anything given to the free tier** — red line #5 makes it permanent. Giving is irreversible; pricing it later is not available.
3. **The fixed reference dialect** — Junior's syllable set becomes the standard's reference; changing it after publication breaks everyone who adopted it.
4. **Publication of the standard** — published is published.
5. **Anything shot at M0** — what is not captured that day is not cheaply recoverable; this deadline governs all sequencing.

**TWO-WAY — revisable on evidence.** Everything else: decide it, build it, instrument it, change it when the numbers arrive.

**The test for a hard case:** ask what it costs to undo in six months. If the answer involves another shoot day, another person's signature, or a promise already made to a student — it is one-way. Only one-way doors get the formal treatment (three-option fork, written input, founder's signature); two-way doors are decided by default.

## 5 · METHOD §5 — the vocabulary rule sheet (R86, approved at INPUT-81)

| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| room, visit, enter | level, stage, unlock-as-earned |
| hold, longest hold | streak, combo, chain |
| best, your best | high score, rank, top |
| cycles played with you | accuracy, score, percentage |
| present, with us, feeding the part | correct, right, perfect, good, miss |
| the part thinned, the part returned | you failed, you lost, try again |
| rhythms met, rhythms carried further | mastered, completed, graduated |
| the map lit | progress bar, completion % |

**Never borrowed from the Caderno, on any play surface:** *received*, *heard*, *confirmed*, *standing*, *acknowledged*. Those words belong to human events and to the master; the play layer never writes into that world.

**The sentence test.** If a string can be read as a statement *about the student* rather than *about the round*, it is wrong, however friendly it sounds.

*(Note the tension the designer must hold: the mandate permits the streak **mechanic**; the vocabulary sheet bars the **word**. Mechanic and wording are governed separately.)*

## 6 · Registers (orientation)

`CHARTER.md` binds absolutely; `METHOD.md` binds procedurally. Series → register: **A/S/G/E** → DECISIONS · **D** → DELTAS · **INPUT** → INPUTS (reserved to a named human; never resolved unilaterally) · **R** → RECOMMENDATIONS · **rows** → LEDGER · **#** → RISKS · **Forks** (lettered) → FORKS · **C** → CONTRADICTIONS. Never reuse a number. Deltas are proposed and the founder ratifies — **operator mandates are the sole exception, operative on issue**. Founder- and Junior-facing questions come as **three options with pros and cons**.
