# Opanijé — Operator Mandate 01: Engagement Design

**Status.** FOUNDER-DECIDED, 2026-07-31. **Operative on issue**, not on ratification — this is an
operator mandate, not a proposed delta. It supersedes every prior instruction named in §2.

**Authority.** Product form on secular material, brand, voice and product shape are the founder's
under v2.0 §12 (RULED). The prohibitions withdrawn below were **PM-derived invariants and adopted
recommendations, never charter**. Withdrawing them requires no ratification from anyone.

**Nothing here touches a red line.** §4 states exactly which four constraints are charter, why they
survive this mandate, and what they permit — which is far more than the withdrawn text assumed.

**Numbering continues the estate.** Deltas from D43 (the session-2 addendum ends at D42). Inputs
from INPUT-62 (ends at INPUT-61). Recommendations from R59 (ends at R58). Ledger rows from 38
(ends at 37).

---

## 1 · The mandate

**The engagement toolkit is open.** Streaks, daily goals, session targets, XP and levels,
celebration moments, variable and delayed reward, loss aversion, unlockables, cosmetic collection,
notification craft, milestone drama, comparison-against-your-own-past, and the rest of the modern
retention repertoire are **permitted and expected** in Release 1.

The prior blanket prohibition — "no streaks, no targets, no scores, no badges, no progress bars,
structurally, not by restraint" — was a PM over-reading. It took a charter rule about **who may
confer standing** and generalised it into a rule about **how the app may behave**. Those are
different questions. The charter answers the first and says nothing about the second.

The withdrawn text also cost us something specific and measurable: M1's two headline exit-evidence
numbers are **first-session completion and day-7 return** (v2.0 §7.4). Those are precisely the two
numbers this toolkit exists to move, and we had ruled ourselves out of the instruments before
measuring anything.

## 3 · The line that replaces them, in one sentence

> **The app never grades your drumming. It may celebrate everything else.**

Musical judgment stays where the charter and the tradition put it: in the student's own ear, in the
agogô, and in the master's Layer-2 attestation. That is R28's real content and it survives.

**Behavioural facts are a different category entirely** — you opened the app, you played eleven
cycles, you came back on day seven, you chose a harder room, you have been here fourteen days
running. Those are the app's to count, dramatize, reward, and chase.

Two structural facts make this line safe rather than merely stated:

- **D26 means the app is deaf.** With no microphone anywhere in Release 1, the app is
  *incapable* of grading playing. The prohibition we needed was already enforced by hardware
  absence, not by restraint.
- **The Caderno is written by human-shaped events**, not by the play layer (§4.1). Standing has its
  own ledger and the engagement layer cannot reach it.

Everything in §1 lives on the near side of that line.

## 4 · The four boundaries that are charter, and what they actually permit

These are red lines. They do not move, and they are not my preference — they are the founder's own
charter, and an investor unwilling to sign them is buying a different company (v2.0 §2).

### 4.1 · Standing is not currency (red line #2)

**Two ledgers, kept structurally apart:**

| | The play layer | The Caderno |
|---|---|---|
| Content | XP, streaks, levels, goals, cosmetics, celebration | *Received*, *Heard*, *Confirmed* |
| Author | The app | Human events and the master |
| Character | Soft, resettable, loud, playful | Permanent, quiet, withdrawal-proof |
| May be gamified | Entirely | **Never** |
| May be bought | No (nothing in the app is bought except access) | No — charter |

**What this permits:** every mechanic in §1, at full volume, on the play side.

**What it forbids:** XP converting into a Caderno entry; a streak influencing *Confirmed*; a badge
or title that reads as a master's acknowledgment ("Recognised," "初 initiated," "Master's Pick");
any purchasable item that confers, accelerates, or implies standing; any surface that presents
gamified rank as lineage rank.

**Practical build rule (R59):** the play layer is a separate, resettable subsystem from day one and
never writes into the Caderno's tables. This is free to do now, while the rail has zero rows, and
expensive to unpick later.

### 4.2 · Nothing is taken back (red line #5)

Loss aversion is permitted, and it is one of the strongest instruments in the toolkit. It may
threaten **streak state, cosmetics, and play-layer progress**. It may never threaten **access**.

**The one Duolingo mechanic that is forbidden here is hearts** — a depletable resource that gates
practice. The free room is promised unchanged forever (E13, red line #5), so a mechanic that stops
someone practising until a timer expires or a purchase clears is a charter breach, not a design
choice.

Permitted: streak loss, streak freeze as a reward, decaying cosmetics, "you lost your nine days,"
end-of-day pressure, the whole family. Forbidden: anything that makes the room itself less
available than it was yesterday.

### 4.3 · Sacred material's form is Junior's (red line #1)

The free rhythm is Junior's (E17), and under S6 the whole battery is Junior. **The engagement layer
therefore wraps his material at the point of maximum public visibility** — which is exactly where
v2.0's risk #2 lives ("gamification read as commodifying the sacred," named as the estate's most
exposed risk).

This is not a veto on celebration. A13 rules these rhythms circulate secularly and are in scope;
INPUT-20's Reading A, if assented, puts the rhythm-as-musical-object outside red line #1. But red
line #1 reserves the **form** of any game mechanic on sacred material to its authority, and a
confetti burst over a toque of Obaluaiê is a form question whether or not anyone intended it as one.

**Operationally this is cheap and it is already on the calendar.** D36 puts the mockup before the
shoot precisely so Junior sees a working thing instead of a description. The engagement layer goes
into that mockup and he rules on the form he can actually see. **INPUT-62**, gathered with INPUT-41
and INPUT-52.

Nothing waits on it: build it, show it, adjust the form if he asks. The mitigation risk #2 already
names — his authorship visible, his name on the partition, his voice through the door — is
strengthened, not weakened, by celebration built out of *his* count-ins (§7.4 of the addendum).

### 4.4 · Real voices, and takedown reaches everything (red lines #4 and #6)

Celebration audio is either Junior's real recordings or non-vocal sound design. **No synthetic
voice, no generated stinger in a master's style, ever** — this is the least ambiguous red line in
the charter. And any gamified surface that references withdrawn material comes down with it: the
cross-system takedown drill in v2.0 §7.4 step 5 now has to reach the play layer's caches and
records too.

## 5 · What this costs, stated so it is not discovered later

- **Release 1 build scope grows.** A play-layer subsystem, notification infrastructure, and
  celebration assets are new engineering that Release 1 did not carry. It lands on top of D37's
  store billing, which was itself new this week.
- **The design brief grows (D48).** R54 commissioned a design language for a propped-up practice
  screen — calm, arm's length, glanceable. It must now also carry celebration, streak state, and
  notification surfaces without breaking the practice screen's stillness. Two moods in one system.
  This is on the Release 1 critical path through R49's lock-and-file.
- **INPUT-39 grows (D49).** Push notifications, engagement telemetry, and retention mechanics aimed
  at a population that includes minors are all disclosure and compliance surface, in both stores and
  under LGPD. This joins the combined counsel brief; it is one of the few brief items that is *not*
  mockup-dependent in substance, only in detail.
- **M1's numbers change meaning.** Day-7 return with a streak mechanic is not the same measurement
  as day-7 return without one. Instrument by mechanic (D50) or we will not know what moved the
  number, and row 24's conversion will be uninterpretable.

## 9 · The room advances the student (RULED, 2026-07-31)

**D28 and R51 are withdrawn.** "The student pulls every lever" is removed as a constraint. The app
may open the next room, and unlocking is available as a reward mechanic in Release 1.

**What follows necessarily, and it is the whole of the constraint:**

- **Advancement runs on behavioural facts, never on quality of play.** Time in the room, cycles
  completed, sessions returned, days present. The app has no microphone (D26) and therefore cannot
  know whether you can play the part — advancing on an inference about your playing would be a
  musical verdict made blind, which is §3's line and §10's item 3. Advancing on what you actually
  did is a fact the app holds directly. The specific threshold is build-side, expressed in
  countable facts, informed by Junior.
- **The student can still move himself, in both directions.** E8 is untouched and R58's reframe
  holds: fewer drums is exposure, more drums is shelter, and moving down is a move rather than a
  retreat. Removing D28 stops manual movement being the *only* mechanism; it does not remove it.
  Reversible on a word if you want advancement to be the app's alone.
- **INPUT-41 changes shape slightly.** Junior and Vanderson were being asked whether a fixed ladder
  is honest teaching. They are now being asked whether a ladder that *advances on its own* is honest
  teaching — a student can be carried into a bigger room before he holds his part. Same question,
  one clause longer, same shoot, no extra cost. Fold it into the mockup Junior sees under §4.3.

## 10 · What still must not be built (replaces addendum §7.8)

Short, and every line traces to a red line rather than to taste:

1. **Anything purchasable that confers, accelerates, or implies standing** (red line #2).
2. **Anything that gates access to what was given** — hearts, lives, energy, timers on the free room
   (red line #5).
3. **Any machine verdict on musicianship** (§3; and structurally impossible under D26).
4. **Synthetic voice of any kind, in any celebration asset** (red line #4).
5. **Badges, titles or ranks that read as a master's acknowledgment**, or that present play-layer
   rank as lineage rank (red line #2).
6. **Any recording of a master that names a price or a product** (R53 — unchanged, and it now also
   means no master's voice inside a promotional or upsell moment).
7. **Any gamified surface that survives a withdrawal** of the material it points at (red line #6).

Everything not on this list is permitted. Where a mechanic is arguable, build it, put it in the
mockup, and take it to Junior under §4.3 rather than to me.
