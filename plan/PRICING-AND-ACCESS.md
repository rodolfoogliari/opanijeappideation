## What governs this file

**The one law** (RULED, charter-level): *access is bought; standing is only earned.* Courses,
memberships, classes and seasons are for sale. Entries in the book, passage between stages, and a
master's acknowledgment can never be purchased (v2.0 §2). Every row below sells access. No row
below sells standing.

**Red line #5** (RULED): the free tier's permanent-access promise holds — the house never takes
back what it gave, and this attaches to anything the app gives away, including the Room's free set
(v2.0 §2). This is the ratchet that makes every free-side decision in this file a ONE-WAY door.

**Red line #2** (RULED): standing is only earned. **R4**, enforced at the API contract layer: a
Confirmed entry carries no link to any payment record, and G0's audit of it is a query rather than
an argument (v2.0 §3.4, §8). The cycle sells presence, cadence and being heard; it does not sell
being confirmed.

**R53** (unchanged and operative): no recording of a master may name a price or a product — and
under Mandate 01 §10 item 6 it now also means no master's voice inside a promotional or upsell
moment. Pricing moves; a recording that names a figure dies the day it does. This is why the
welcome and the invitation are shot price-free and product-free (M0 shoot manifest; Mandate 01 §3).

**Door labels.** Assigned from the estate's own reasoning where it states one; derived from D83
item 2's one-way list otherwise and marked "(derived)". D83 names the one-way list explicitly:
consent scope, **anything given to the free tier**, the fixed reference dialect, publication of the
standard, and anything shot at M0.

## 1 · The pricing principle — the commons is free; scarcity is priced

**D73 (ADOPTED).** *The commons is free; scarcity is priced.* Supersedes R48's depth-only shape and
gives INPUT-27 its rule; the list itself moves to INPUT-80 (Addendum 04 §5, §10, §11).

The principle follows from G25 (FOUNDER-DECIDED): rhythms already readily available online — Ijexá
is the named example — are free. What is given away is material that was **never scarce**, which is
what makes giving it permanently safe under red line #5. What is priced is what is actually scarce:
depth, rarity, this specific battery, and the human ear.

### 4.2 · The strain, stated plainly

**Risk #29 (OPEN) — the cycle strains the one-obligation rule.** A monthly live event is a standing
commitment on the two people who have the least slack. The founder's operating rule is *one new
standing obligation at a time*, and v2.0 already recorded Release 1 as "the first thing in this plan
that strains the rule" before the cycle was added to it (v2.0 §1; Addendum 04 §14).

*Mitigation, as the estate states it:* INPUT-82 is a go/no-go **with eyes open**; the format may
start as small as one hour; nothing else in the plan depends on it except Fork K's strongest answer
(Addendum 04 §14).

**The estate does not assume this away, and neither does this file.** The membership is sold from
day one, so the promise to a paying member starts before the format has been lived through even
once.

## 11 · Conflicts and gaps recorded

Recorded rather than resolved by preference, per the estate's own discipline. Where two documents
disagree, the later state is carried and the earlier one is named.

**D81 and D37 against D15 — the in-app subscription rail.** D15 rules that **M3 launches on the web
rail only**, because the subscription is a from-zero build and doing it twice — once on the web, once
through two stores' billing plus a compliance surface nobody in the estate has touched — is not
affordable at M3; A4's dual rails "stay ruled and arrive when M4's cash funds them" (v2.0 §5.1,
§7.6). Addendum 04 §5 sells the cycle "as the membership from day one on the rails already committed
(Pix; store billing per D37)" — which places a recurring membership on store billing at **Release 1**,
ahead of M3. Addendum 04 neither cites nor withdraws D15. **Carried at the later state under the
authority order; D15 named here because the cost it priced has not been re-priced.** Routed to
INPUT-82 (price and format) and INPUT-40 (the accounts that make in-app billing possible at all).

**The cycle's shape — D4 and v2.0 §4.3 against D81.** v2.0's cycle is **asynchronous**: the call, a
two-week practice window, then the response, with a **real live roda quarterly** framed explicitly as
a **gift** so that missing it churns nobody. D81's Release-1 form is a **monthly live event**,
streamed. The estate does not state how the two relate — whether the Release-1 live event replaces
the async cycle, precedes it, or becomes the quarterly gift made monthly. Recorded as an unresolved
relationship, not as a contradiction, because Addendum 04 describes an operational Release-1 form and
v2.0 describes a built M3 product. **Routed to INPUT-82's "format".**

**INPUT-16 was asked about a different cycle.** INPUT-16 asks Junior whether the **async** cycle works
as a roda, and is gathered at the shoot because he is in the room (v2.0 §7.2, §7.6). D81's monthly
live event is a different commitment and a different form, and no input has been opened for his
assent to *it* — INPUT-82 puts the decision to the founder and Junior together, which is the nearest
thing the estate has. Recorded as a gap.

**E15's stated ground is void.** E15 (FOUNDER-DECIDED) says the 1:1 is absent from Release 1, "and
forced by §4.1's zero-commerce ruling." **D37 removes the zero-commerce ruling.** The decision stands

holds, and E15 has not been revisited since. Recorded so nobody later reads the parenthetical as live
reasoning. The 1:1 remains hand-booked through Track B.
