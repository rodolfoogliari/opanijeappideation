## What M0 is, and why it governs sequencing

M0 is the production sprint — the day the cameras roll on Junior. It turns the earmarked
≈R$8k–10k into the founding house's first sellable assets and into the Room's raw material, with
every consent and rights instrument executed **before the cameras roll** (v2.0 §7.2; exact scope
and quotes INPUT-2; scope movable until the founder closes it, A10 — RULED).

**It is the estate's only hard external deadline, and it is a deadline of irrecoverability rather
than of calendar.** What is not captured that day cannot be recovered cheaply, and in most cases
cannot be recovered at all: you cannot extract isolated strokes from a performance, you cannot lock
a standalone solfejo to a battery afterwards, and you cannot re-consent a master from a weak
position. This is why the whole estate sequences against it — the mockup runs before the shoot
(D36) so Junior rules on working things rather than descriptions, the rough prototype recording
runs before the mockup (R76) so the audio half exists, and every item that costs him a shoot day is
an input rather than an assumption (S7).

**Every item shot at M0 is a ONE-WAY door.** D83 item 2 names the one-way list explicitly — consent
scope, anything given to the free tier, the fixed reference dialect, publication of the standard,
and **anything shot at M0** — and the shoot is the item that makes the others urgent. Rows below
carry ONE-WAY unless the estate itself states the item is recoverable later; where a door label is
inferred rather than stated it is marked "(derived)".

**Two standing rules attach to the whole day.** No recording without an executed consent instrument
ships — no exceptions, red line #6 in operating form (v2.0 §7.2). And no recording of a master may
name a price or a product, because pricing moves and a recording that names a figure dies the day
it does (R53; PLAN-AMENDMENTS A4).

**Three documents have carried a section titled "The M0 shoot, as it now stands," each superseding
the last** — Addendum 01 §9, Addendum 03 §12, Addendum 04 §8. This file consolidates all three and
restores the detail that Addendum 04's table folds into summary lines, because a manifest that is
not shootable is not a manifest.
