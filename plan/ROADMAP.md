## 4 · First build quarter

Four workstreams. The first two are the game; the third makes it legible; the fourth is the arc.

**4.1 · The instrument.** Tap door → screen drum, **one surface** (R74), scheduled audio per G10,
D77's real one-shots for out-of-window and wrong-zone strikes, and D78's fade-and-rejoin. The stroke
sample library (D58) exists so the drum is honest, not so it is pretty.

**4.2 · The echo renderer.** Muting states over solfejo recorded locked to the battery (R63). No new
capture, no new audio engineering — the loop is nearly free precisely because it is render-time
muting. Shaped by **INPUT-78** and gated on **INPUT-79**.

**4.3 · The post-round and personal-best layer**, with the vocabulary sheet applied (R86, `METHOD.md`
§5). Three facts, one best, monotonic display. This is where classification leaks back in if the
strings are written carelessly, so the strings are specified before they are written.

**4.4 · The repertoire map** (D75) — one object, the state space lit.

**4.5 · The cycle's thin surfaces** — appointment, calendar, replay, Caderno entry — **if INPUT-82
goes**. Thin is the whole point: the app carries the appointment, not the submission. Submission runs
over ordinary channels outside the app, keeping D26 (no microphone) fully intact.

**4.6 · Instrumentation** per D50, extended to three new things: replays *after* the best is secure,
pressure and guilt items, and fade-comprehension. These exist to convert ledger rows 48–51 — or to
falsify them.

**Also in the quarter, from the standing plan:** the play layer as a separate resettable subsystem
that never writes into the Caderno's tables (R59 — free now while the rail has zero rows, expensive to
unpick later), the entitlement rewrite, accounts, and the Pix rail live before Release 1 (S3).

## 5 · M1 — and what it now means

**M1 measures a product *with* a core game and an appointment, and *without* the extrinsic toolkit.**
This amends R81, and it must be **recorded as exactly that, dated, before the numbers exist** — a
baseline defined after the fact is not a baseline.

**Two numbers judge every game element:** first-session completion and day-7 return.

**What converts here:** ledger rows 48–51, at the pilot and at M1.

- 48 — the echo loop is experienced as teaching, not testing
- 49 — visible sustain and personal bests increase voluntary replay **without pressure or guilt**
- 50 — the fade reads as honest information, not punishment
- 51 — the commons-free / scarcity-priced boundary converts free players to the paid line (M2, first
  paid cohort)

**Row 49 is the one that governs Fork L.** If it fails, D79's visible sustain is reversed without cost,
because the underlying facts are collected either way.

**The extrinsic toolkit — streaks, XP, goals, loss aversion, unlockables — remains available past the
door** under Operator Mandate 01, and **stays untested until a deliberate M2 experiment**. It is not
forbidden; it is unmeasured, and shipping it inside M1 would make M1 unreadable.
