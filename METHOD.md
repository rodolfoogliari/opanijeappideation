# Opanijé — Method

**Status.** Operative. Constituted 2026-08-03 under **D83** (Game Addendum 04 §7), which restructured
the estate: charter and backlog split, one-way/two-way door labels on every open item, and a standing
monthly decision hour replacing input accumulation.

**What this file is.** How the estate works — the labels, the doors, the numbering, the meeting
rhythm, the wording, and the session format. It replaces the "Working method" sections that were
duplicated at the end of every continuation prompt.

**What binds and what does not.** `CHARTER.md` binds absolutely. This file binds procedurally: break a
rule here and the estate becomes unreliable, not void.

## 1 · Epistemic labels

Every claim in this estate carries a label. **Epistemic discipline is load-bearing** — the labels are
what keep a hopeful sentence from turning into a planning input three documents later.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RULED** | A founder or governance decision. Needs no market validation. |
| **MASTER-CONFIRMED** | A claim about the tradition, its pedagogy, or its material, confirmed by the tradition's named authority. For Ketu material: Junior. Sits **above** FOUNDER-FACT for tradition claims and closes the corresponding verification input. Added by **D71**, 2026-08-03. |
| **FOUNDER-FACT** | A fact about the tradition, market, or community only the founder holds, asserted in session and treated as true (FF1–FF4). |
| **FOUNDER-DECIDED** | A product decision taken by the founder in session. |
| **ADOPTED** | A previously open recommendation now operative as plan-of-record, reversible at ratification review. |
| **PM-RECOMMENDED** | The contracted product manager's advice. Carries no force until adopted. |
| **PM-ASSERTION** | A PM claim from general knowledge, unverified. Requires founder, Junior or counsel confirmation before anything rests on it. |
| **DERIVED** | A consequence that follows necessarily from something already decided, stated so it is visible rather than discovered. |
| **REPORTED** | An asset asserted as existing; not independently audited. |
| **ASSUMPTION** | A modeled number awaiting measurement. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | A causal claim that must be tested. |
| **MEASURED** | Supported by completed transactions or observed cohorts. |
| **VERIFIED-EXTERNAL** | A third-party rule or fact checked against source on a stated date, carrying its own re-verification requirement. |
| **VERIFIED-INTERNAL** | A fact about the company's own estate, checked against the running system on a stated date by inspection rather than by reading documentation. All such findings to date are **self-audit, not independent audit**. |
| **FALSIFIED** | A ledger status: a claim checked against reality on a stated date and found untrue. **Retained** on the ledger rather than deleted, with the work that would make it true named beside it. |

**As of 2026-08-03, nothing in this estate carries MEASURED.** That is the single most important fact
about the evidence base, and it is why `registers/LEDGER.md` exists.

**No ledger row upgrades without dated evidence.** Never upgrade a label to make a plan read better.

**The research documents use a second label set** (DIRECT-ADJACENT, TRANSFER, MECHANISTIC,
HYPOTHESIS). Treat it as a research-provenance sub-scale, not a parallel system, and say which scale
you are on when you cite `research/`.

## 2 · Door labels

**D83 item 2.** Every open item in `BACKLOG.md` carries a door label. Only one-way doors get the
formal treatment — the three-option fork, the written input, the founder's signature. Two-way doors
are decided by default and revised on evidence.

**ONE-WAY — expensive or impossible to reverse.** The estate names five classes:

1. **Consent scope.** What the consent covers cannot be widened after the shoot without going back to
   the people who signed it.
2. **Anything given to the free tier.** Red line #5 makes it permanent. Giving is irreversible;
   pricing later is not available.
3. **The fixed reference dialect.** Junior's syllable set becomes the standard's reference; other
   masters then carry their own inside the frame. Changing the reference after publication breaks
   everyone who adopted it.
4. **Publication of the standard.** Published is published.
5. **Anything shot at M0.** The cameras stop. What is not captured that day is not cheaply
   recoverable, and this is the deadline that governs all sequencing in this estate.

**TWO-WAY — revisable on evidence.** Everything else. Decide it, build it, instrument it, and change
it when the numbers arrive. Fork L is the model: D79's visible sustain is ratified now and reversed
without cost if ledger row 49 fails, *because the underlying facts are collected either way*.

**The test for a hard case:** ask what it costs to undo in six months. If the answer involves another
shoot day, another person's signature, or a promise already made to a student — it is one-way.

## 3 · The monthly decision hour

**D83 item 3.** A standing hour with Junior, synchronised with his cycle, replaces input accumulation.

**The rule: no input ages past thirty days.**

The estate's failure mode was inputs piling up between sessions until a shoot date forced a rushed
batch. The decision hour exists so that the register in `registers/INPUTS.md` is drained on a rhythm
rather than on a deadline.

**How the hour runs.**

1. Open with anything that closed since last time — what his last answers unblocked.
2. Take the ONE-WAY doors first, in irrecoverability order, not in importance order.
3. Each item as a plain-language question with **three options, pros and cons for each**.
4. Anything not reached is carried, dated, and reported as carried — never silently.

**What the hour is not.** It is not a status meeting and it is not consultation. Junior's formal input
is a governance gate (`CHARTER.md` §7). An input answered in the hour is a ruling; an input not asked
is not an assumption.

## 4 · Numbering and the registers

**Session prose is no longer the register of record** (D83 item 1). The registers in `registers/` are.
Session documents remain in `sessions/` as derivation history — the reasoning that produced each
decision, which the register rows deliberately do not carry.

| Series | Register | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| A, S, G, E | `registers/DECISIONS.md` | Founder rulings, session decisions, game decisions, experience decisions |
| D | `registers/DELTAS.md` | Proposed changes to ratified material |
| INPUT | `registers/INPUTS.md` | Questions reserved to a named human |
| R | `registers/RECOMMENDATIONS.md` | The PM's advice, and whether it was taken |
| rows | `registers/LEDGER.md` | Load-bearing claims and what would convert them |
| # | `registers/RISKS.md` | Risks and their stated mitigations |
| Forks | `registers/FORKS.md` | The open product questions, lettered |
| C | `registers/CONTRADICTIONS.md` | Places two documents said incompatible things, and the disposition |

**Rows are appended, never rewritten.** A row's *status* may change; its *claim* may not. A superseded
row stays, marked superseded, pointing at what replaced it. A falsified row stays, marked falsified,
with the work that would make it true beside it.

**Deltas are proposed; the founder ratifies.** Operator mandates are the single exception — they are
founder-issued and operative on issue, not on ratification.

**Open inputs are reserved** to the founder, Junior, or counsel. Never resolve one unilaterally. Where
a terse answer has to be interpreted, the interpretation is written down as a **confirm-or-correct**
row with the cost of being wrong stated — the estate's standing practice, and the reason Addendum 03
§2 and Addendum 04 §2 exist.

**Numbering state as of 2026-08-03** is carried in `registers/NUMBERING.md`. Continue the estate's
numbering; never reuse a number, even for a withdrawn item.

## 5 · The vocabulary sheet

**R86, approved at INPUT-81.** Written once, applied everywhere. The wording is where classification
would leak back into a product that has carefully designed it out, so the wording is specified rather
than left to whoever writes the string.

| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| room, visit, enter | level, stage, unlock-as-earned |
| hold, longest hold | streak, combo, chain |
| best, your best | high score, rank, top |
| cycles played with you | accuracy, score, percentage |
| present, with us, feeding the part | correct, right, perfect, good, miss |
| the part thinned, the part returned | you failed, you lost, try again |
| rhythms met, rhythms carried further | mastered, completed, graduated |
| the map lit | progress bar, completion % |

**Never borrowed from the Caderno, on any play surface:** *received*, *heard*, *confirmed*, *standing*,
*acknowledged*. Those words belong to human events and to the master, and the play layer never writes
into that world (`CHARTER.md` §4).

**The sentence test.** If a string can be read as a statement *about the student* rather than *about
the round*, it is wrong, however friendly it sounds.

## 6 · Session format

**The founder's format.** Read the situation → ask for an OK → present questions with **three options
each, with pros and cons**. Plain language, and plainer when asked — a request for simpler must be
honoured without losing the substance.

**The founder is here to conceptualize.** Technical decisions go to the build side. Technical findings
surface only when they force a product-level fork — and when they do, **surface them with the cost
stated, not the conclusion assumed**. The audio-latency question dissolved once the platform's own
capability flags were read; an afternoon of phone-buying had been recommended before that check was
made. Check whether the platform already answers it.

**Two numbers judge every game element:** first-session completion and day-7 return. Argue against
those, not against whether something is fun.

**The honest caveat, to be carried into any engagement argument.** The research base narrows the design
space; it does not choose the product. Gamification effects are positive on average, heterogeneous,
and often small. There is almost no high-quality evidence for a single-player, microphone-free,
culturally governed percussion practice product. **Nothing should be built because it worked in a
paper** — and the product still has to be measured.

## 7 · Closing a session

A session that changes anything must leave the estate consistent:

1. Register every new decision, delta, input, recommendation, ledger row and risk in `registers/`.
2. Update `BACKLOG.md` — new open items with door labels, closed items struck with what closed them.
3. Update `registers/NUMBERING.md`.
4. File the session's reasoning in `sessions/` as a dated document. It is history, not the register.
5. If anything constitutional moved, amend `CHARTER.md` **and say so in the delta's own text**.
