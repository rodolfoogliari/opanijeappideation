# Opanijé — The Charter

**Status.** Constitutional. RULED, charter-level. Established by `plan/PRODUCT-GOALS-VNEXT-CONSOLIDATED-V2.md`
§2 and §12; extended by Operator Mandate 01 §4 and §10, Game Addendum 01 §7 and §11, Game Addendum
02 §15, and Game Addendum 04 §3.2, §3.5 and §7. Constituted as a standing document on 2026-08-03
under **D83**.

**What this file is.** Everything in the estate that is genuinely constitutional, in one place, short
enough to be read before every session. Nothing here is a preference, a recommendation, or a design
choice. Everything here binds until the founder — and, on sacred material, Junior — says otherwise
in writing.

**What this file is not.** It is not the plan, not the backlog, and not a record of reasoning. The
plan of record is `plan/PRODUCT-GOALS-VNEXT-CONSOLIDATED-V2.md`. Open work lives in `BACKLOG.md`.
Numbered history lives in `registers/`. The reasoning that produced each rule lives in `sessions/`.

**The distinction that governs this file.** A milestone missed is a milestone missed. A red line
crossed is the company ended.

---

## 1 · The one law

> **Access is bought; standing is only earned.**

Courses, memberships, classes and seasons are for sale. Entries in the book, passage between stages,
and a master's acknowledgment can never be purchased.

---

## 2 · The six red lines

To be written into the corporate documents before any first check clears.

1. **Each tradition's sacred boundary is governed by that tradition's own named authority.** For Ketu
   Candomblé material: Junior, always. No game mechanics on sacred material unless its authority
   designs the form himself; no sacred iconography in product art without approval.
2. **Standing is only earned.** No entry, passage, honour, or acknowledgment is ever purchasable.
3. **Supply is curated.** Masters enter by vouched invitation through a bench; no self-serve door.
4. **No synthetic voice, ever.** Every voice is a real recorded human; no cloned masters, no
   machine-composed calls "in his style."
5. **The free tier's permanent-access promise holds.** The house never takes back what it gave — and
   this attaches to anything the app gives away, including the Room's free set (R24/R30).
6. **Consent and credit are structural.** A teller's withdrawal takes his material down; credit is
   permanent; price worlds never cross-wire.

An investor unwilling to sign these is buying a different company, and the answer to that investor
is no.

**Red line #6 has no operating form yet.** There is no cross-system takedown anywhere in the estate
(ledger row 30, FALSIFIED, verified 2026-07-30). This is **not a violation** — no consented material
has been published, so nothing has been promised that cannot be honoured. It is a hard requirement
before Release 1 ships any consented material, and it is carried in Release 1 scope as the red line's
operating form rather than as a feature. Whether the promise must reach copies already on devices is
the founder's and counsel's interpretive question (**INPUT-47**); under D27 it does not gate
Release 1.

**Red line #5 is why the free tier is a one-way door.** Anything given away is given permanently. Every
decision that adds to the free tier is therefore irreversible and is labelled ONE-WAY in `BACKLOG.md`.

## 3 · The partition

Junior's five-value sacred partition (R20, ADOPTED; enforcement language corrected by D17) governs
what may be done with each item of material, item by item:

> *playable-inside · gamifiable (recognition/vocalization only) · teachable-but-not-gamifiable ·
> archive-only · unrecordable*

The partition is **enforced at the API contract layer**, not by convention. Junior's name is on it and
it is stated in his voice.

The second value — *gamifiable, recognition/vocalization only* — was close to dead weight until the
vocalization ruling (G2–G6) built the game it was written for. Vocalization, tap-along and the screen
drum are all recognition-and-vocalization forms; they do not seat a student in an ensemble role inside
a recorded liturgical battery. Material Junior would never mark *playable-inside* may therefore be
marked *gamifiable* and have a real game to be gamifiable into — in a form the partition itself
already sanctions rather than one the product invented.

Item-by-item capture happens at the M0 shoot (**INPUT-21**) and is irrecoverable context: the rulings
can come later, being in the room with him cannot.

## 4 · The two ledgers

Structurally separate subsystems, from day one. This is architecture, not policy — the play layer
never writes into the Caderno's tables (R59).

| | The play layer | The Caderno |
|---|---|---|
| Content | Rooms visited, holds, personal bests, cosmetics, celebration | *Received*, *Heard*, *Confirmed* |
| Author | The app | Human events and the master |
| Character | Soft, resettable, loud, playful | Permanent, quiet, withdrawal-proof |
| May be gamified | Entirely | **Never** |
| May be bought | No — nothing in the app is bought except access | No — charter |

**What the separation forbids:** play-layer progress converting into a Caderno entry; a streak
influencing *Confirmed*; a badge or title that reads as a master's acknowledgment; any purchasable
item that confers, accelerates, or implies standing; any surface that presents play-layer rank as
lineage rank.

The play ledger is **resettable by design**. That property was built early and became load-bearing
when the game's records were designed against it (D75).

## 5 · Consent and credit

Red line #6 states the rule. This is its operating form, and it is charter because a consent failure
is not recoverable by any amount of later care.

**Consent is captured before the cameras roll.** Every consent instrument is recorded **in the
teller's own voice**, and every rights document is executed before the shoot — not after, not
alongside.

**The hold condition, verbatim (v2.0 §7.2):**

> **Any recording without an executed consent instrument does not ship. No exceptions; red line #6
> in operating form.**

**The scope question is the cheapest irreversible item in the estate.** Whether the M0 consent
instruments as drafted license **interactive and game use** is **INPUT-22** — rank 1 of six by
irrecoverability, near-zero cost to fix now, and not recoverable later without re-consenting masters
and reopening commercial terms from a weak position. Isolated per-instrument stems are **INPUT-23**,
rank 3, and impossible without re-recording.

**Credit is permanent, and it is structural.** Credit survives the material: a Caderno entry is
**withdrawal-proof** — it survives the disappearance of the material it points at, because material
comes down and credit does not. Masters carry permanent structural credit alongside production done
for them.

**Withdrawal must actually reach everything.** Red line #6's operating form is a **cross-system
takedown** — short-lived signed tokens, asset removal at the CDN, entitlement revocation — proven by
a drill, and it must reach the play layer's caches and records too. A takedown that stops at the CDN
is not a takedown.

**This does not exist yet.** There is no cross-system takedown anywhere in the estate (ledger row 30,
**FALSIFIED**). It is a hard requirement before Release 1 ships any consented material, and whether
the promise must reach copies already on devices is **INPUT-47**, the founder's and counsel's.

**One thing this Charter deliberately does not state: a play-surface credit rule.** E2 put the real
named musicians on screen, lighting up as each joins. D38 voided it — under S6 the battery is Junior
alone, layered, and "the real musicians" cannot light up one at a time when one man was recorded four
times. The replacement is **INPUT-60**, still open. Addendum 04's presence lamp (E21) then builds on
E2 as though it were live. That conflict is registered as **C23** and routed to INPUT-60; until it is
settled, the Charter carries credit at red line #6's wording only.

## 6 · The grading constitution

**D76 — ADOPTED 2026-08-03.** One principle replaces every prior per-surface rule on evaluation.

> **Facts may drive sound; nothing may drive a sentence.**
> **Sustain is visible, precision is audible, judgment is human.**
> **The game grades the round; the ear grades the playing; the master grades the player.**

The app may operate mechanically on facts and render musical consequences. It may **never** express an
evaluation in words, numbers, meters, tiers, stars, letters or percentages about the quality of
playing — and it may **never** say anything at all about the player.

Four things the estate had collapsed into one banned category, now kept distinct:

| | What it is | Permitted? |
|---|---|---|
| **Measurement** | A strike occurred at time *t* in zone *z* — a fact | Yes |
| **Feedback** | What that meant, musically — information | Yes, in the audible channel |
| **Grading** | How this round went — a summary of play | Yes, post-round, self-referenced (D79) |
| **Classification** | What this student *is* — a claim about a person | **Never** |

Only the fourth is dangerous, and it is the only one the charter forbids: machine claims of readiness,
correctness, mastery, or standing. A game grades the run, never the player. The run ends; you go
again; nothing was said about you.

**There is no fail state anywhere in the product** (D78). The battery never stops for you; your part
thins when you stop feeding it and returns when you strike again. You cannot lose; you can only be
more or less present in the music.

**The voice is never measured, in any mode.** Absolute, unchanged, and structurally true today under
D26 (no microphone). It stays true if a microphone ever ships.

## 7 · The vocabulary rule

**R86 — the wording is where classification would leak back in, so the wording is specified.**
The working sheet is `METHOD.md` §5. Founder approval is **owed at INPUT-81, which is open** — the rule binds as plan-of-record meanwhile.

Play-layer words are **spatial and musical**: rooms, visits, holds, bests, cycles, presence.

Play-layer words are **never the Caderno's**. Barred from every play surface: *received*, *confirmed*,
*heard*, *mastered*, *level*, *rank*, *graduate*, *unlocked-as-earned*, and any synonym that reads as
a master's acknowledgment.

## 8 · Authority — who decides what

**The founder (RULED, v2.0 §12).** Every price, product name, brand and voice; which shape leads;
which traditions and masters are invited; every business term; and form decisions on **secular**
material.

**The benches (RULED, v2.0 §12).** Everything sacred, under the named authority of its tradition — in
the founding house, Junior. Including lineage language, the partition item by item, standing
per-episode review of the archive, and *Confirmed* allocation (A5).

**A charter red line cannot be narrowed from one side.**

**Junior's formal input is a governance gate, not a consultation.** No sacred-material ruling moves
forward without it.

**MASTER-CONFIRMED** (D71) is the label for a claim about the tradition, its pedagogy, or its material
confirmed by the tradition's named authority. It sits above FOUNDER-FACT for tradition claims.

## 9 · What must not be built

Cumulative and consolidated. Every line traces to a red line or to a ruling, never to taste.
Items 1–7 from Operator Mandate 01 §10; 8–10 from Game Addendum 01 §11; 11 from Game Addendum 02 §15,
**amended in scope by D79**.

1. **Anything purchasable that confers, accelerates, or implies standing.** (Red line #2)
2. **Anything that gates access to what was given** — hearts, lives, energy, timers on the free room.
   (Red line #5)
3. **Any machine verdict on musicianship.** (Structurally impossible today under D26)
4. **Synthetic voice of any kind, in any asset.** (Red line #4)
5. **Badges, titles or ranks that read as a master's acknowledgment**, or that present play-layer rank
   as lineage rank. (Red line #2)
6. **Any recording of a master that names a price or a product** — and therefore no master's voice
   inside a promotional or upsell moment. (R53)
7. **Any gamified surface that survives a withdrawal** of the material it points at. (Red line #6)
8. **No measurement of the voice, ever, in any mode.** The app measures taps and screen strikes. The
   voice is the one part of the practice that is between the student and the tradition.
9. **No share or export of any recording containing a master's material** until **INPUT-71** is
   answered. A takedown that stops at the CDN is not a takedown. (Red line #6)
10. **No screen-drum surface shipped without Junior having seen it.** (Red line #1) Building it unshown
    is permitted and expected; shipping it unshown is not.
11. **No timing accuracy readout, in any form, on any surface** — no tiers, no labels, no percentage,
    no "perfect/good/close," no per-strike marks.
    **Amended by D79 (RATIFIED 2026-08-03):** per-strike feedback remains binary, generous and
    audible-only; a **post-round aggregate of those binaries** — sustain at cycle granularity, plus
    per-setting personal bests — is visible. Accuracy-style readouts remain barred on every surface.

Also barred, restated so the plan is safe (Addendum 04 §3.5): stars, letters, tiers,
"perfect/good/miss" labels, fail screens, streaks with loss, person-levels, readiness or mastery
claims, public rank, comparison between students (G17), purchasable repair, and any game result
touching the Caderno.

**Everything not on this list is permitted.** Where a mechanic is arguable, build it, put it in the
mockup, and take it to Junior — not to the product manager.

## 10 · Two standing rules that are not red lines but bind sequencing

**The standing resource rule.** Always choose the option cheaper in people and more expensive in
Junior's own hours.

**The one-obligation rule.** One new standing obligation at a time. Applied whenever scope additions
are evaluated. It is genuinely strained by the monthly cycle (D81) and that strain is recorded as
**risk #29** rather than assumed away.

## 11 · How this document changes

The Charter changes only by explicit founder ratification, and — where sacred material's form is
touched — only with Junior's sign-off under red line #1.

Everything else in the estate is **proposed** until ratified, and proposals are recorded as numbered
deltas in `registers/DELTAS.md`. A delta that would change this file must say so in its own text.

**Where the Charter and any other document disagree, the Charter governs.**
